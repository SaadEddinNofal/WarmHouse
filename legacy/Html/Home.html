About The Project

Warm House was created to support responsible animal adoption and improve the lives of pets by helping them find safe and loving homes.

The platform also provides a dedicated marketplace for pet supplies, including food, toys, accessories, healthcare products, and everything needed for a happy and healthy life.

Our mission is to build a trusted community where adopters, animal owners, shelters, and pet lovers can connect together.
